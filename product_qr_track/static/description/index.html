Product QR Track

Track customer engagement and location from product label scans

Our product QR track extends the Dymo product label and adds QR code unique to each product. When customer scans the QR code they are redirected to the trackable link. The system automatically captures valuable analytics about the scan.The system automatically captures valuable analytics about the scan, helping you understand your customers better and optimize your marketing strategy.

Moreover, product qr track provides a template with QR code of the product, which can be called in any of the product's report. This template adds a QR code with trackable link to capture customer visits and valuable analytics.
