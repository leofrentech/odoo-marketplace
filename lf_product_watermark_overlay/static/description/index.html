How it works

Three steps, and you choose exactly which products take part.

1 Set your watermark

In Settings, turn watermarking on and pick a logo or text, its position, size and opacity. A live preview shows the result before you save.

2 Choose products

Tick Eligible for Watermark on the products you want branded. It's off by default, so nothing is watermarked until you decide.

3 Upload as usual

New photos on eligible products are watermarked automatically. For existing photos, Regenerate All Images backfills them in the background.

Frequently asked questions

Will enabling watermarking change all my product images at once?

No. Only products marked Eligible for Watermark are watermarked, and that box is unticked by default, so you decide exactly which products are branded.

Can I get my original photo back?

Yes. The untouched original is stored alongside the watermarked image. Remove Watermark, or unticking Eligible for Watermark, restores it immediately.

Does it watermark the images on my online shop?

Yes. The shop shows the product's own images, so they carry the watermark. For the Extra Product Media gallery, add the free companion module Product Image Watermark Overlay - Website Sale: once it's in your addons, it installs itself alongside eCommerce. Videos are left alone.

Does this app work with Odoo Enterprise and Community?

Yes — it works with both Odoo Enterprise (on-premise and Odoo.sh) and Community. Odoo Online (cloud) does not allow installing third-party apps, so this module can't be installed there.
