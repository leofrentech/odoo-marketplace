The companion to Product Image Watermark Overlay for eCommerce. It brings the same watermarking to the Extra Product Media gallery shown on your website's product pages. It installs automatically once both eCommerce and Product Image Watermark Overlay are installed — there's nothing to configure.

What it adds

✓ Same rules as your product images

Gallery images follow the product's Eligible for Watermark setting and your company-wide watermark.

✓ Apply and remove together

Add/Update Watermark and Remove Watermark cover the gallery along with the product's main and variant images.

✓ Background backfill

Regenerate All Images also watermarks existing gallery images, in the background.

✓ Videos left alone

Media items that are videos keep their thumbnail untouched; GIFs and SVGs are skipped too.
