Which products can be personalized? +

Any product you sell on your website. Give it its own areas or a shared design template; an area can also be limited to one variant, for example a different mockup per colour.

How is the price calculated? +

The product price, plus the personalization fee, plus the extra price of each area the customer actually customized. It is added to the unit price of the order line. Pricelist discounts apply to the product price, not to the personalization.

What does my production team receive? +

For each customized side: a transparent PNG of the printable zone at 2×, 4× or 6× the editor resolution (Print File Quality in Settings), an SVG file and the customer's original uploaded images. Download Artwork on the order gives everything in one ZIP. The designs are also shown on the quotation and order PDF.

Can a customer change a design after ordering? +

Yes, when you reject it with a note. They fix it from their customer portal and send it back for review. The order price does not change, so they can't add a paid area they did not order.

Which images can customers upload? +

PNG, JPG and WebP, up to the size you set in Settings (15 MB by default). The editor warns them when an image is too small to print sharply.

Can I limit what customers do? +

Yes, per product: which tools they get (text, image upload, clipart, shapes), the maximum text length, the colours they can choose, the fonts, and whether a design is required before buying.

Do Google Fonts send data to Google? +

Only if you activate them. Google Fonts are archived by default; you can upload your own font files instead, which are served by your own website.

Who can see a customer's design? +

The customer (from their cart, their portal or the order link) and your team. Uploaded files are checked: only image formats are accepted and SVG print files are cleaned before they are stored.

Does this app work with Odoo Enterprise and Community? +

Yes — it works with both Odoo Enterprise (on-premise and Odoo.sh) and Community. Odoo Online (cloud) does not allow installing third-party apps, so this module can't be installed there.
