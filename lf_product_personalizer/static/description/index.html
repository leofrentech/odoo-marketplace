Questions Answered

Everything you want to know

Can customers design any type of product?

Yes! The module works with any product you configure—from clothing and accessories to business cards and promotional items. You control which products offer personalization.

What file formats do customers need?

Customers can upload JPG, PNG, and SVG. The system prepares files automatically for production.

How do I prevent designs in certain areas?

You can define restricted zones in product setup. These areas stay protected for brand elements.

Can sales team see the designs?

Yes — every design appears in the sales order with a preview and downloadable production files.

Does it work on mobile devices?

Yes! The interface is fully responsive for phones, tablets, and desktop devices.

What happens after a customer designs a product?

The design attaches to the cart item and flows into the sales order. Production receives high-resolution print-ready files automatically.
